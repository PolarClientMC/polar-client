Overview

Players

Coins

Search users to manage coin balances. sharky124321345 and CraftyCanyon are included by default. Players earn 10 coins an hour while the game is open. The server counts it, so nobody can change their coins from their own PC.

Partnered servers

Manage partnered servers stored on the server. Logo/banner must be https://raw.githubusercontent.com/... URLs; Discord must be a discord.gg / discord.com/invite link.

Cosmetic prices

Cosmetics are free for everyone until you give them a price here. Code-only cosmetics cannot be bought with coins (Haze and special cosmetics are always code-only). Priced cosmetics must be bought before they can be equipped.

Codes

Codes hand out a cosmetic (usually a special one) to whoever redeems them in the launcher.
